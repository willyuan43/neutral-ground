How it hooks young players

Built for a 15-year-old’s first minute

  • The boss first: a storm flash-forward, then a rewind to 2020
  • You are someone: walk your character, plant with your own hands
  • Never lost: a task list, one hint line, a go-here arrow
  • One big button that changes with where you stand
  • Instant feedback: sprouts, pops, level-ups, confetti
  • Collect and replay: 18 badges, island decorations, field notes, 8 endings
  • Phone-ready, no install, 6–8 minutes
Our first playtests failed: players didn't know where they were or what to do, and a year passed in two clicks. So we rebuilt the first minute the way hit games do. You see the storm first. You control a character. One button, a task list and an arrow always show the next step. Every action gives instant feedback, and there is always something to collect or another ending to find.
