Nothing on the island is invented. The terrain is USGS lidar, land cover is ESA WorldCover, paths and places come from OpenStreetMap, buildings from DataSF, soils from USDA. Every day you replay is NOAA's recorded rain at downtown San Francisco, and the storm uses hourly airport records.
