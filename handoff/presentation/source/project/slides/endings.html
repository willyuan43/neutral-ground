No random rolls. Every ending card is the same size: Neutral Ground is the challenge goal, not the only right answer.

There are eight endings, decided by the model on 30 September 2023 from three things: did the grassland come back, did the storm's biggest hour stay under the line, and how many neighbours got what they asked for. Locked endings tell you how to reach them, which is the main reason players replay.
