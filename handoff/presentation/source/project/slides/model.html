The model

A small teaching model computes every outcome

Seedlings

A daily soil-water balance (FAO-56 style) on the real rain. Survival comes from the days of severe thirst in the first year.

Storm runoff

Each 5 m cell makes runoff hour by hour (SCS curve number). Water follows the steepest path down the lidar to a garden or the drain.

Gardens and tanks

Buckets that fill, overflow, soak in and drain. Water let out later through a pipe still counts as reaching the drain.

Land check

Inspired by Land Degradation Neutrality: each kind of land against doing nothing, no offsetting between kinds. Erosion is a relative index.

Not modelled: flooding downstream, pipes, groundwater, fire, species or animal numbers. Tests check that every ending can be reached.

The model is deliberately small and explainable. Seedlings live or die by a daily soil-water balance on the recorded rain. Storm runoff is computed hour by hour for every 5 m cell and routed down the real terrain. Rain gardens and tanks are simple buckets. The land check borrows the idea of Land Degradation Neutrality. It is a teaching version, not an official assessment.
