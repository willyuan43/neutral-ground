Neutral Ground

A real storm is coming. You have three autumns to get a San Francisco canyon ready.

A 6–8 minute browser game set in the real Glen Canyon Park, played through its real 2020–2023 weather

Neutral Ground is a short browser game for 15–24-year-olds. You play a new canyon keeper in a real San Francisco park. You get three autumns, in the real weather of 2020 to 2023, to get the canyon ready for the real New Year's Eve 2022 storm.
