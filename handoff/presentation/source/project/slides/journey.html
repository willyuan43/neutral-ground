The player journey

From the first tap to the ending in 6–8 minutes

1 · The storm first, then a rewind to 2020

2 · Your mission: three stars

3 · Walk and plant with your own hands

4 · Leo waits on the shortcut: go and talk

5 · The drought year: water them or not?

6 · Result, level-up, one lesson

7 · New Year’s Eve: call the peak hour

8 · The ending and your stars

Here is the whole run. The storm flash-forward and the mission card answer where am I and why. You walk and plant yourself, then meet Leo. The first year is the real 2021 drought, so the watering decision matters. Each year ends with a result and a level-up. After three autumns the New Year's Eve storm plays hour by hour, and you get one of eight endings.
