People, not just plants

Four neighbours, four asks that pull against each other

Maya, volunteer: bring the native grass back on the slope

Leo, day-camp counselor: shade for his campers, keep their way down

Coach Rivera: save roof water for the field

Ms. Chen, downhill: less water rushing to the drain in the storm

The puzzle: closing the shortcut helps Maya but cuts off Leo. Rerouting it serves both. Help 3 of the 4 to earn a star.

Restoration is a people problem too. Each fictional neighbour asks for something, and their asks conflict. Maya wants the trampled shortcut closed; Leo's campers use it. Players discover that rerouting the trail satisfies both. The neighbours are labelled as simulated characters and only react to what the model computed.
