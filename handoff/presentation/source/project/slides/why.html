Why a game

Young people care about the land, and many feel powerless

59%

of 10,000 people aged 16–25 in ten countries were very or extremely worried about climate change

Over half

said they felt powerless and helpless about it

Our answer: agency

In Neutral Ground your own choices, in a real place, change what its real weather does. You see it happen, then try again.

Source: Hickman et al., “Climate anxiety in children and young people”, The Lancet Planetary Health, 2021

A big survey of 10,000 young people found most are worried about climate change and more than half feel powerless. Lectures don't fix that feeling, but agency does. So we built a game where your own decisions, in a real place you can find on a map, change the outcome in that place's real weather.
