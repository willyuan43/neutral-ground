What players learn

Every lesson comes from a choice you watch play out

You doYou see, in real weatherThe lesson
Sow native grassCovered ground holds; bare soil washes on storm daysCover is the first defence
Water, or waitIn the 2021 drought most unwatered seedlings dieTiming is water
Plant trees on the grasslandGreener slope, smaller grasslandRestore the right land
Keep, close or reroute the trailA trampled path stays bare; a reroute keeps a way downPeople and land both count
Build a rain garden or tankThe storm’s peak hour drops; held water still drains laterSlow the peak; later isn’t less
Guess before a yearThe model checks your guessPredict, then test with data

The land check borrows the idea of Land Degradation Neutrality: avoid, reduce, reverse, and never offset one kind of land with another.

Nothing is a quiz. Players learn by doing something and watching the real weather respond. Three moments are built to surprise: the drought year killing unwatered seedlings, trees making the slope greener but not better, and a rain garden lowering the storm's peak.
