Honest by design

Six rules we never break

No invented numbers

Every number comes from the model or observed data, tagged model or observed on screen

Only what we compute

No flooding, dust or water quality drawn, and no claims about animals or species

Fiction is labelled

The neighbours are simulated, and the ending pictures are marked as AI illustrations

Teaching tokens

Budgets are tokens, sweat hours and water, never real dollars

Inspired by LDN

The land check borrows the idea; it is never an official SDG 15.3.1 assessment

History is history

The 2006 management zones are a past plan, not a current approval or funding

A game about real land has to be honest about what it knows. Every number on screen is tagged as model output or observed data. We only draw outcomes the model computes. Characters and AI pictures are labelled as fiction. The budget is in teaching units, and the land check is inspired by, not equal to, the UN's Land Degradation Neutrality indicator.
