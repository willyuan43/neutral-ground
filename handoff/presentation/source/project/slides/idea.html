The idea

A real canyon, its real weather, your choices

Real place

Glen Canyon Park, San Francisco: 5 m lidar terrain, real land cover, the real paths, buildings and Rec Center

+

Real weather

Daily rain from Oct 2020 to Sept 2023: a 230 mm drought year, then the New Year’s Eve 2022 storm, 139 mm in one day

+

Your choices

Plant, water, keep or reroute a trail, build a rain garden. A teaching model computes what survives and what reaches the drain.

Same weather for everyone; different choices, different endings. That is what makes the lesson stick.

Three ingredients. A real place you can visit. Its real recorded weather, including a drought year and a record storm. And your choices. The model only computes what happens; it never makes up the weather or the place.
